Frequently Asked Questions

How do I delete my account?

Open Eleven, go to Profile → Edit Profile, scroll to the bottom, and tap "Delete Account." This permanently removes your account and all associated data.

How do I restore a purchase?

On your iPhone, go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions to view or manage your Eleven subscription directly through Apple.

How do I change my narrator voice?

Go to Profile → Edit Profile, and select a different narrator voice from the options available.

How do I turn off daily reminders?

Go to your iPhone's Settings → Notifications → Eleven, and turn off notifications there.
