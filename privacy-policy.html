Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 September 2026

Eleven is operated by Nexa Labs Pte. Ltd. ("we," "our," "us"), a company registered in Singapore. Eleven is a personalized manifesting audio story app. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, who we share it with, and the choices and rights you have. By using Eleven, you agree to this policy.

1. Who Can Use Eleven

Eleven is intended for users 18 years of age or older, or the equivalent age of majority in your jurisdiction. If you are under this age, you may only use Eleven with the consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian, who agrees to these Terms on your behalf. By using Eleven, you represent that you meet this requirement, or that you are the parent or legal guardian of a minor and consent to that minor's use of the Services.

2. Information We Collect

a) Account information

Eleven creates an account for you automatically. This may be an anonymous account tied to your device, or, if you sign in with Apple, a unique identifier from Apple (and your name/email only if you choose to share them — Apple lets you hide your email).

b) Profile and story information you provide

During onboarding and while using the app, you may voluntarily share personal details used to generate your stories, including: your name, gender, age, general life satisfaction, your goals, your reasons for wanting them, your desires, relevant experience, personal blockers, and — optionally — the name and relationship of a supportive person in your life, and your preferred narrator voice. Some of this information (for example, details about personal struggles or relationships) may be considered sensitive in certain jurisdictions. We only process it because you have voluntarily provided it, and solely to generate your personalized stories.

We do not sell this information, and we do not use it for advertising or targeted marketing.

c) Content you create

Custom prompts you type into the "manifest" feature, and the stories and audio generated for you, are stored so you can revisit them.

d) Usage and device information

We automatically collect limited technical information needed to operate the app: which stories you play, app-open events, crash and performance diagnostics, device type, operating system version, and reminder/notification settings. This information does not directly identify you.

e) Subscription and payment information

If you subscribe, payment is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. Apple holds your actual payment details (such as card information) — we never receive or store them. Our subscription management provider, RevenueCat, receives transaction records (what was purchased and when, linked to your account) so we can determine which features you have access to. RevenueCat does not process payments and does not hold your payment card details.

3. How Your Information Is Used to Generate Content

Eleven uses third-party artificial intelligence services to create your personalized experience:

  • Story text is generated by sending your profile details to Anthropic's Claude AI model, which writes a personalized story.
  • Story audio/narration is generated by sending the story text (not your raw profile data) to Fish Audio's text-to-speech service.

These providers process this information solely to generate your content and do not use it to build advertising profiles about you.

7. Your Rights and Choices

Revoking consent and managing your data

  • Edit your information: you can update your profile details at any time within the app.
  • Withdraw consent / delete your account: you can permanently delete your account and all associated personal data directly within the app, in Settings → Account → Delete Account. This removes your profile, stories, and account information from our systems within a reasonable period, except where we are legally required to retain certain records.
  • Notifications: you can disable reminders at any time in your device's notification settings.

If you are in the EEA, UK, or Switzerland (GDPR)

Nexa Labs Pte. Ltd. is the data controller for your information. You have the right to: access the personal data we hold about you; correct inaccurate data; request deletion; restrict or object to processing; and receive your data in a portable format. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

If you are a California resident (CCPA/CPRA)

You have the right to know what personal information we collect, request deletion, and opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information. We do not sell or share your personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. To exercise your rights, contact us below.

8. Data Retention

We retain your profile and story data for as long as your account remains active, so you can access your story history. If you delete your account, we delete your associated personal data within a reasonable period, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate business records (such as financial transaction logs).
